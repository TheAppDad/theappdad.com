The App Dad Android app

If you use the Android app The App Dad (package com.theappdad.app) or the testing platform at app.theappdad.com, the full policy is here:

The App Dad (Android) — Privacy Policy

That page explains what we collect (Google sign-in, campaign data, questionnaire answers, notification tokens), who we share it with (Google, Supabase, Stripe, Tremendous, Resend, Anthropic, Cloudflare), and how to delete your account.

Delete your The App Dad account

  1. Email hello@theappdad.com from the Google account email you use in the app.
  2. Write “Delete my The App Dad account”.
  3. We will delete or anonymise personal data linked to your account within 30 days, except limited records we must keep for tax, fraud prevention, or an unresolved dispute.

There is not yet a delete button inside the Android app. Email is the supported method. Full details: account deletion section.
